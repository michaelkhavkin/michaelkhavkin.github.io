I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Interacting With Technology (IWiT) lab in the School of Industrial & Intelligent Systems Engineering at Tel Aviv University, advised by Prof. Eran Toch.

I study usable privacy where privacy technology meets human decisions: how protections change what people are willing to disclose, and how much people can see of what a system has already learned about them.

Research Agenda

To align the data practices of computing systems with the privacy preferences of the people they affect, in a setting where protection is uneven and what counts as adequate differs across people and across systems, from social media platforms to national censuses. This requires both seeing what a system does with personal data and being able to tune it, which defines two lines of inquiry.

(1) Where the information flow is specified in advance, as with differential privacy, I examine how protections are configured in practice and how their parameters can be elicited and tuned so that the privacy-utility trade-off people are handed matches the one they would choose.

(2) Where the flow emerges during use, as in LLM-based systems, users cannot yet see what has been disclosed, inferred, or retrieved about them, and I develop interactive explainability mechanisms that make those flows visible enough to act on.

Methodologically, I use algorithm design, quantitative and qualitative user studies, and system prototypes.

Tools

LLM Inferential Privacy Mirror

A browser-based tool that shows users, in real time, what an LLM-powered chatbot can infer about them from their conversations and lets differential privacy limit that exposure. Built as a research prototype to study inferential privacy leakage in AI assistants.

Code ▶ Demo

Differentially Private Election Campaign Management

A web-based interface for managing election campaigns with differential privacy guarantees. Enables campaign managers to encourage potential voters to vote and to analyze voter data statistics on election day, while ensuring that individual voter information remains protected through differential privacy mechanisms.

Code ▶ Demo

FL-DP-XAI Viewer (Explainability in Differentially Private Federated Learning)

An interactive visualization tool for exploring the trade-off between differential privacy and explainability in federated image classifiers. Designed to support clinicians in understanding how privacy constraints affect model transparency in clinical decision-support systems.

Code

GPTalyze - Text Snippet Analysis with ChatGPT

A Jupyter notebook pipeline for large-scale NLP analysis of short text using the ChatGPT API. Supports sentiment analysis, emotion detection, and topic extraction via zero-shot prompting. Developed as a research instrument for studying public discourse around generative AI on social media.
